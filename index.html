Trading Cheat-Sheet for Beginners
RISK MANAGEMENT 101
🛑 Setting a Stop Loss
Below/Above Structure

Long trades → place SL below the most recent swing low. Short trades → place SL above the most recent swing high, not at a random round number.

Volatility-Based (ATR)

Use 1.5x–2x the Average True Range (ATR) as buffer, so normal price noise doesn't stop you out prematurely.

Never Move SL Away From Entry

Once set, only move your stop loss in your favor (to lock profit) — never widen it to "give the trade more room."

⚖️ Core Risk Rules
1Risk 1–2% per trade

Never risk more than 1-2% of your total account on a single position — use the Position Size Calculator above to size correctly.

2Minimum 1:1.5 Reward:Risk

Only take trades where potential reward is at least 1.5x your risk — check with the Risk/Reward Simulator above before entering.

3Max daily loss limit

Set a hard stop for the day (e.g. -5% of account) — if hit, close the platform. Revenge trading destroys accounts faster than bad setups.

4Journal every trade

Log entries, exits, and reasoning in the Trading Journal above — your past trades are the best teacher you have.

🔄 Reset Trading Desk

Clears your Trading Journal entries, resets the Win-Rate Simulator and Position Size Calculator back to default values. This does not affect live market data widgets.

Investing in US / International Stocks from India
BEGINNER GUIDE
📋 How It Works
1LRS Route

Under RBI's Liberalised Remittance Scheme (LRS), resident Indians can remit up to $250,000 per financial year to invest abroad, including in US stocks.

2Choose a Platform

Use a SEBI-registered broker with US stock access, or a direct US broker offering India-friendly onboarding — compare fees, forex spread, and withdrawal ease.

3Fund & Invest

Transfer INR, it gets converted to USD (forex conversion charges apply), then you can buy fractional or full shares of US-listed companies.

⚠️ Key Things to Know
Tax Compliance

Gains are taxed in India as capital gains (foreign assets), and you must disclose foreign holdings in your ITR under Schedule FA — non-disclosure has penalties.

TCS on Remittance

Tax Collected at Source (TCS) applies on LRS remittances above a threshold — check the current rate before transferring, as it affects your effective cost.

Currency Risk

Your returns depend on both the stock's performance AND the INR/USD exchange rate — a stock can rise in USD terms but your INR return could differ.

Alternative: Index Funds

If direct stock-picking feels complex, Indian mutual funds that invest in US index funds (like Nasdaq 100 feeder funds) offer simpler exposure without LRS paperwork.
